Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Will the bot make stuff up?

A FAQ or RAG bot answers from content you give it and is told to say “I don’t know, here’s how to reach a human” when it’s out of scope. Hallucination risk drops sharply when the model is grounded in retrieved text instead of free-styling. We test the edges before launch.

Do you need our passwords?

Tiers 1 and 2, basically no — a FAQ bot holds zero credentials, a wizard needs calendar/email access. Only the agentic tier touches real systems, and there we use scoped, least-privilege keys stored encrypted, with every action logged so you can audit what it did.

How much will the AI cost per month to run?

For most small-business bots, single-digit to low-double-digit euros a month — see the slider above. It climbs with heavy context (RAG) or tool-use (agentic). Prompt caching makes repetitive FAQ traffic dramatically cheaper. It’s usually the smallest line on the invoice.

Can it speak Greek properly?

Yes — that’s the point of defaulting to Claude. It handles Greek tone, formal/informal register, and code-switching mid-sentence. Greek runs a touch more tokens than English, which the estimate already assumes.

We already have a website. Do we need a new one?

No. The chatbot drops onto your existing site with one script tag. We only build a site if you want one or the current one is holding the bot back.

What if we outgrow the tier we start on?

That’s the plan. Every tier upgrades into the next without a rebuild — a FAQ bot becomes a RAG bot when your content grows, a wizard becomes agentic when you’re ready to let it take actions. You only pay the difference.

Do we own it?

Yes. The build is yours. The managed-service subscription is optional — drop it whenever, the bot is still yours. No lock-in.
