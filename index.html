FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a website take?

A landing page can be done in about 5–7 days. A full business website typically takes 1–2 weeks. Growth packages with more pages and features take 2–3 weeks.

What do I need to provide?

Your business information, logo (if you have one), photos you'd like to use, and an idea of what you want the site to achieve. I handle the rest — structure, design, copy suggestions, and build.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. If you already have a website but it feels outdated, slow, or unclear, it can be redesigned with a stronger structure and better conversion flow. Same pricing applies.

Will my website work well on mobile?

Yes. Mobile-first design is a core priority. Every site is built and tested to look great and convert well on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. LMLabs can help set up your domain, hosting, and get everything connected so your site goes live properly.

What if I don't like the design?

Every package includes revision rounds. I show you a mockup or preview before building the final version, and we iterate until you're happy. Nothing launches without your approval.

Do I own the website?

Yes. You own everything — the design, the code, the content. There's no lock-in and no required monthly fees. Optional maintenance is available if you want ongoing support.

How do I pay?

50% deposit to start the project, 50% before launch. For larger projects (€2,500+), we can split it into 3 payments. Bank transfer or PayPal accepted.
